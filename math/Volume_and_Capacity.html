1. Fundamental Formulas & Methods

Method A: Measuring the Sides of 3D Objects

Estimate first. Then put the ruler's 0 tip at the start of an edge and read the other end.

 

Box: measure the length, width and height. Cylinder: measure the height and the diameter (radius = half).
Going-back edges look shorter. Turn the ruler along one: it turns purple and shows the true length.
Units: cm for boxes, m for tanks and rooms. 1 L = 1000 cm³ and 1 m³ = 1000 L.

1. Fundamental Formulas & Methods

Method B: Volume, Capacity and Cost Formulas

Volume is the space inside an object (cm³ or m³); capacity is how many liters it holds:

Box Volume = l × w × h
Cylinder Volume = πr² h
Capacity 1 L = 1000 cm³ · 1 m³ = 1000 L
Wall Area = 2(l + w) × h
Cost = quantity × price per unit

Standard Problem-Solving Steps for Volume, Capacity and Cost:

  1. Identify the object and measure each dimension with the ruler. Write the units.
  2. Choose the formula: box V = l × w × h, cylinder V = πr²h.
  3. Convert to liters: 1000 cm³ = 1 L, 1 m³ = 1000 L.
  4. Multiply by the quantity and the price. Label the answer (₱).
